CEP 3.1.4 - By Winternite, The Amethyst Dragon, Pstemarie, CEP Teams, and NWN Community (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwnee/hakpak/combined/cep-3-community-expansion-pack)
A large quantity of free custom content especially creature and placeable models for use on the server.

Gigaschatten's PW engine V4 - Arelith edition - By Gigaschatten & Mithreas (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/script/gigaschattens-pw-engine-v4-arelith-edition)
This provides the base engine for the entire persistent world. The version in use on the server is heavily modified with new features available in the enhanced edition.

DMFI Wands & Widgets 1.09 - By Tsunami282 (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/script/dmfi-wands-widgets-109)
The DMFI wands are in use as tools to assist DMs with creating stories.

Customize Character Override HAK (CCOH) - by kamiryn (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/hakpak/original-hakpak/customize-character-override-hak-ccoh)
This is used to provide players with armor customization options. Modified for use on the server.

Complete Rural/City - By Armor Darks (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/hakpak/combined/complete-ruralcity)
A tileset used for various locations in the Prasinos area.

'Doubled-Jointed' Leg Models - By Ryuujin (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/hakpak/original-hakpak/doubled-jointed-leg-models)
Used to supply furry races with digitigrade legs.

Tail Pack - By Ryuujin (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/hakpak/original-hakpak/tail-pack)
Used for its fluffy tail model.

Fox/fennec ears and tail VFX - By Illy, Nuclear Nibbles, Ryuujin (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/model/foxfennec-ears-and-tail-vfx)
Used for its hair color channel ears and tail. Ears modified to be more catlike for use with certain Nujiit subraces.

Gold Half-Dragon - By Ryuujin (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/hakpak/original-hakpak/gold-half-dragon)
Silver Half Dragon - By apeiron (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/model/silver-half-dragon)
Modified to create a younger version of the Veydran race.

Ancarion's Skinmesh Animals 2024 Redux - By Ancarion, Buddha (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwnee/model/ancarions-skinmesh-animals-2024-redux)
Modified to create new cat colors and to add clothes to cat models for use with a certain Nujiit subrace.

Body Tailor (Body Modifications) - By Bloodsong (https://neverwintervault.org/project/nwn1/script/body-tailor-body-modifications)
Modified to only permit certain changes to the player's body.

Karandas model By Ossian Studios (Model present in NWN:EE files and Aurora toolset.)
Karandas texture modified to create adult Veydran race.
